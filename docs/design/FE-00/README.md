# FE-00 visual review concepts

Status: **concept only — not approved for implementation**.

These five ImageGen boards test one coherent responsive direction for the
Women AI Network frontend:

1. `01-world-discovery-concept-v1.png` — public-letter discovery.
2. `02-letter-reading-concept-v1.png` — long-form reading and mutual connection.
3. `03-profile-privacy-concept-v1.png` — profile preview and per-field visibility.
4. `04-circle-detail-concept-v1.png` — governed circle, asynchronous notes and chat entry.
5. `05-unified-messages-concept-v1.png` — private and circle conversations.

## Shared direction under review

- Warm paper, deep ink, muted teal, terracotta and sand hairlines.
- Editorial serif content with restrained sans-serif controls.
- Desktop top navigation: World, Connect, Circles, Messages, Me.
- Mobile bottom navigation for the same five spaces.
- Letters and reading remain primary; no swipe feed, popularity metrics or match score.
- Circle Notes stay distinct from group chat; group chat lives in Messages.
- Privacy and safety actions remain available without dominating the interface.

## Do not copy literally

- Generated copy, icons, botanical marks and illustrations are placeholders.
- The heart-like Interests icon in board 03 is rejected as too dating-like.
- The wordmark, logo and footer slogan are not approved brand assets.
- The user approved the overall navigation, density, palette and editorial tone before WEB-01
  implementation. Individual generated illustrations and the final brand identity remain
  replaceable concept assets.
